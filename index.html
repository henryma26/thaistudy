🔍 Step-by-Step: How to Define the Final Tone

Step 1
Check Tone Mark

If there is a mark (่ ้ ๊ ๋), follow the Mark Rules immediately.

Step 2
Identify Class

Look at the initial consonant. Is it Mid, High, or Low?

Step 3
Check Ending

Is it a Live (long/sonorant) or Dead (short/stop) ending?

Result
Final Tone

Combine the 3 factors using the reference below to get the correct pitch.

👄 Vowel Length & Tone Impact

Long Vowels

Acts like a Live ending. Tone is stable.

อา อี อู เอ โอ
Short Vowels

Acts like a Dead ending. Tone "cuts off."

อะ อิ อุ เอะ โอะ

⚖️ Live vs Dead Differentiation

LIVE (Continuous)

Ends with Long Vowel OR m, n, ng, y, w.

DEAD (Abrupt)

Ends with Short Vowel OR k, t, p.

"If you can hum the ending, it's Live. If it stops your breath, it's Dead."
